Purpose Statement
Why this document exists

PrimeTurf was conceived as an artificial turf and landscaping business. The VDOS process was not designed to validate that idea — it was designed to destroy every assumption it rests on and discover the highest-value opportunity hidden behind it.

Six assumption stress questions. Twenty-four assumptions destroyed. One structural truth confirmed: PrimeTurf is not an installer business. It is a specification-layer control system — with architects and developers as its distribution layer, installers as execution, and the founder as standards and warranty authority.

"The window to establish specification control in Gauteng and Cape Town's premium outdoor surface market is open now. No dominant player has claimed this position."
