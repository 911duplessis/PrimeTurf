## 🌱 About PrimeTurf

PrimeTurf is a premium outdoor transformation company specializing in **high-quality artificial grass supply, installation, and landscape solutions**.

The brand focuses on creating beautiful, low-maintenance outdoor environments that enhance residential properties, estates, commercial spaces, and modern lifestyles.

From concept to installation, PrimeTurf combines design, craftsmanship, and durable materials to deliver outdoor spaces built for South African living.

---

## 🎯 Vision

To redefine outdoor living by creating premium, sustainable, and functional environments that extend the home beyond its walls.

PrimeTurf transforms ordinary outdoor areas into lifestyle spaces where families, businesses, and communities can connect with nature without the maintenance demands of traditional landscaping.
