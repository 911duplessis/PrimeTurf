What Does Artificial Grass Cost in South Africa?

The honest answer: artificial grass installation in South Africa in 2026 ranges from R150 to R700+ per square metre, fully installed. That wide range exists because "artificial grass" covers everything from budget retail rolls to engineered luxury systems — and because site preparation requirements vary significantly between properties.

For a quality installation that will last 10 to 15 years, budget R280 to R500 per square metre all-in. That's the range where you get UV-stabilised product, proper drainage engineering, and a workmanship guarantee that means something.

What Is Included in Installation Costs?

A professional artificial grass installation is not just rolling out a carpet. A PrimeTurf full installation includes the following:

Site Preparation

Removal of existing lawn or surface material, ground levelling, and compaction. This alone can account for R30–R80 per m² depending on how much material needs to be cleared and how much levelling is required.

Drainage Sub-Base

A properly installed drainage layer (typically crusher dust or aggregate) is compacted and levelled to ensure rainwater and pet waste drain efficiently. This is what separates a professional installation from a DIY job — without proper drainage, artificial grass becomes a swamp in Gauteng's summer rain season.

Weed Barrier Membrane

A geotextile weed barrier is laid over the compacted base before the turf is installed. This prevents weed growth from penetrating the turf surface and ensures the sub-base remains stable over time.

Turf Installation

Professional laying of the turf, including seam joining (for larger areas), perimeter securing, and infill material (silica sand) to raise the pile and anchor the turf.

Final Brushing and Inspection

The finished surface is brushed to raise the pile and the installation is inspected before client sign-off and warranty documentation is issued.
