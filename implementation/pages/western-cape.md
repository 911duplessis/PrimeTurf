### Premium Materials
Every Western Cape installation uses UV-stabilised, salt-air resistant synthetic fibre. Engineered for the region's coastal exposure, intense sun, and wind conditions — maintaining colour and softness year-round.

Full site preparation including soil grading, compacted aggregate sub-base, weed barrier membrane, and drainage engineering designed for the Western Cape's winter rainfall pattern. Proper preparation ensures long-term surface stability and efficient water flow.
