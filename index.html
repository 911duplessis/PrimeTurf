Our Process

The secret to a long-lasting lawn starts beneath the surface.

Our professional 7-step sub-base process ensures exceptional drainage, durability, weed prevention, and a natural look and feel that lasts for years.

  1. 1

    Jeyes Fluid & Coarse Salt Mix

    Helps keep weeds and bugs away.

  2. 2

    G5 Soil

    Expertly levelled for a stable foundation.

  3. 3

    Crusher Dust

    Provides excellent drainage and strength.

  4. 4

    Thin Dust Cement Layer

    Binds the base together.

  5. 5

    Cement Edges

    Creates the perfect surface for high-quality glue adhesion.

  6. 6

    Professional Artificial Turf Installation

    Precision installed for a flawless finish.

  7. 7

    Silica Sand

    Improves durability, drainage, temperature control and delivers a realistic feel.
