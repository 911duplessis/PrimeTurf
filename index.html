How It Works

The PrimeTurf Process

Every PrimeTurf project follows a refined four-stage process — from first consultation to final walkthrough.

1

Consultation

We assess your space on-site, understand your vision, and define the project scope.

2

Design & Quote

Our design team prepares a before/after visual mockup. You receive a transparent, itemised quotation.

3

Site Preparation

Our installation team levels and prepares the site — surface preparation, drainage levelling and premium turf installation.

4

Final Inspection
