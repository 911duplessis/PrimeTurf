Frequently Asked Questions
Is artificial grass allowed under Gauteng water restrictions?+
Yes. Artificial turf requires zero irrigation and is fully compliant with Rand Water, Johannesburg, and Tshwane Level 1 restrictions.
Do I need HOA or ARC approval?+
Most Gauteng gated estates require ARC approval for exterior landscaping. PrimeTurf manages this submission on your behalf as standard — the only installer in Gauteng that does so.
How long does installation take?+
Most 50 to 150 square metre residential installations complete within one to two days. Larger estate projects typically within 48 hours.
What warranty does PrimeTurf offer?+
12-year UV stability and durability warranty on all turf, plus 24-month installation workmanship guarantee. All terms provided in writing before work begins.
How much does installation cost in Gauteng?+
Pricing depends on area size, surface preparation, and product grade. We provide a detailed fixed written quote following a free site assessment.
Does artificial grass drain properly in summer storms?+
Yes. All installations use fully permeable backing and a prepared sub-base meeting SANS 10400 stormwater requirements.
