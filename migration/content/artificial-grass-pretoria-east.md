#### How does PrimeTurf handle Pretoria's clay soil? 
Clay soil is common throughout Pretoria East and requires specific sub-base preparation to ensure proper drainage. PrimeTurf installs a compacted aggregate drainage layer before laying the weed barrier and turf, preventing waterlogging and surface instability that clay soils would otherwise cause.
