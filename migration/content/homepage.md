## Our Process — The PrimeTurf 7-Step Sub-Base System

"The secret to a long-lasting lawn starts beneath the surface." Our professional 7-step sub-base process ensures exceptional drainage, durability, weed prevention, and a natural look and feel that lasts for years.

1. **Jeyes Fluid & Coarse Salt Mix** — Helps keep weeds and bugs away.
2. **G5 Soil** — Expertly levelled for a stable foundation.
3. **Crusher Dust** — Provides excellent drainage and strength.
4. **Thin Dust Cement Layer** — Binds the base together.
5. **Cement Edges** — Creates the perfect surface for high-quality glue adhesion.
6. **Professional Artificial Turf Installation** — Precision installed for a flawless finish.
7. **Silica Sand** — Improves durability, drainage, temperature control and delivers a realistic feel.
