Referral Reward
5%

Of the total job value, paid in cash to the connector once the client's deposit is received and the job is confirmed.

Closing Bonus
R500

Fixed bonus paid when the installation is completed and signed off — on top of the referral reward above.

  • The 5% referral reward only activates once this agreement is signed — referrals made before signing are not eligible.

  • Payment is made once the client's deposit clears — no reward is paid on unconfirmed or cancelled jobs.
  • The closing bonus is paid separately once the installation is complete and signed off by the client.
  • There are no fees, no joining costs, and no obligation to sell — connectors only introduce.
