Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

PrimeTurf ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.primeturf.co.za. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or contact us for services, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013, South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request a quote, or submit an enquiry form. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address or area
  • Project details and specifications
  • How you heard about us

We may also collect non-personal technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site, collected via analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide quotations
  • Schedule site consultations and installations
  • Send project updates and warranty documentation
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp on +27 76 804 8868, your communications are subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy (whatsapp.com/legal). We use WhatsApp solely to communicate with you regarding your enquiry or project.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfil legal obligations. Project records are typically retained for the duration of the warranty period (8 years) and a reasonable period thereafter for accounting and legal purposes.

5. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/)

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and analyse traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.
