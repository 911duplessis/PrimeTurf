Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026 · Effective date: 11 June 2026

PrimeTurf ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal information of our clients and website visitors in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a data subject.

1. Who We Are

PrimeTurf is an artificial grass installation business operating across Gauteng and Cape Town, South Africa. Our website is primeturf.co.za. For data-related enquiries, contact us at 076 804 8868 or via WhatsApp at wa.me/27768048868.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, phone number, and the message content you provide.
  • WhatsApp messages: your WhatsApp display name and phone number when you initiate contact via our WhatsApp link.
  • Quote calculator: project details you enter (surface type, area, location, grade). No personally identifiable information is collected via the calculator — only anonymised usage events.

We also collect non-personal, anonymised analytics data via Google Analytics 4 (GA4), including pages visited, session duration, device type, and general geographic region. This data cannot identify you personally.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a free site consultation.
  • To provide a quote and follow up on outstanding proposals.
  • To communicate project scheduling and installation updates.
  • To issue warranty documentation following completed installations.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, nor do we sell, share, rent, or transfer it to any third party for commercial purposes.

4. Third Parties We Work With

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) — processes contact form submissions on our behalf. Their privacy policy applies to form data in transit.
  • Google Analytics 4 — collects anonymised website usage data. Data is processed by Google LLC under their standard terms. You may opt out at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — if you contact us via WhatsApp, Meta's privacy policy applies to that communication.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may collect anonymised request data per their standard terms.

All third parties are required to process your data in accordance with applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to manage your enquiry, provide services, and fulfil warranty obligations. Enquiries that do not proceed to installation are typically deleted within 12 months. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see §7).

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies placed by Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymised usage statistics. These cookies do not identify you personally. By continuing to use this website, you consent to the placement of these analytics cookies. You may delete or block cookies via your browser settings at any time — this will not affect your ability to use the site.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Objection: object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at justice.gov.za/inforeg if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in §8. We will respond within 30 days.
