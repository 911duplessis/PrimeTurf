Observe Patterns

Hidden patterns in what recovered and what didn't

Pattern 1 — metadata alone didn't bring rankings back. Four location pages were already rebuilt on Wix with materially better titles and descriptions than the originals (Sandton, Johannesburg, Hyde Park, Edenvale — internally marked "PROTECTED + ENHANCED"). None of the four appears anywhere in Semrush's current ZA keyword data for this domain. Good on-page copy without redirects, schema, and preserved link equity hasn't been enough on its own.

Pattern 2 — the migration favoured brochure pages over money pages. Every general/informational page (About, Services, Blog, Portfolio, Contact) has custom SEO on Wix today. The pages most likely to have driven commercial, bottom-of-funnel search traffic — the suburb/location pages — are the ones still missing. The content that was easiest to rebuild got rebuilt first; the content that earns revenue didn't.

Pattern 3 — this is a "stopped showing up" problem, not a "can't win this market" problem. Every missing location keyword below carries a Keyword Difficulty of 11 or under (out of 100) in Semrush's ZA index. The one page still holding a position (Cape Town, KD 11) is on page 4 for a term this easy — with real content and a working URL, page 4 is not the ceiling.
