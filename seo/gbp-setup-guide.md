## Step 1: Create / Claim Your Profile

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account you want to manage this from.
2. Click "Add your business to Google"
3. Search "PrimeTurf" — if a listing already exists, click "Claim this business". If not, click "Add your business".
4. Follow the verification flow. **Choose phone verification** (fastest) — Google will call or SMS the number on file. If phone isn't available, request a postcard (5–14 days to deliver to your Gauteng address).
