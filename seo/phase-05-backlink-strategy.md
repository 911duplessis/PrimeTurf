## 6. South African-Specific Notes

- **POPI Act**: All outreach emails are B2B to editorial/business contacts, not consumers — POPI's opt-in requirements apply differently. Still include a brief unsubscribe note.
- **Journalist relationships**: South African trade media is a small community. Personal relationships with editors at Visi, House & Leisure, and SA Architect (established through consistent, high-quality pitches and genuine project photography) are more valuable than any single placement.
- **Afrikaans media**: `Die Burger`, `Rapport`, and `Landbouweekblad` are viable targets for the Winelands and Western Cape market, especially for a kunsmatige gras / water restrictions angle.
- **Seasonal timing**: Cape Town water crisis coverage spikes from October through March (summer/pre-summer). Schedule media pitches with the Cape Town drought angle for September–October to align with editorial planning cycles.
